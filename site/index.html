Every sign, ready to animate.

Motion capture of American Sign Language for Blender, Unreal Engine, Mixamo and Rigify. Free for anything, under CC0.

      Recorded by Studio Galt with an Xsens suit and StretchSense gloves, cleaned in Blender. Every file is CC0: use it however you like.

      Signs are made by a hearing signer and are not a substitute for a Deaf-led translation. If one looks wrong, use “Report a problem” on that sign and it will be re-recorded.

      Missing a sign? Request it, or add a 👍 to one that's already been requested. The most-wanted signs get recorded first.
